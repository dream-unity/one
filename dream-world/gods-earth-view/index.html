A quick introduction

Welcome to God’s Earth View

Explore Earth on an interactive globe. Follow aircraft and satellites, listen to radio from other places, and watch public cameras. Choose what interests you and explore at your own pace.

  1. Start with a place

    Open LOCATION, search for a city or landmark, and choose a result. Drag the globe to look around. Scroll to zoom, or move with one finger and pinch with two on a touch screen.

  2. Add something to explore

    Open DATA LAYERS with its + button. Try one layer first, such as aircraft, satellites or earthquakes. Select a marker for more information.

  3. Listen, watch and check the roads

    Choose Live radio, CCTV cameras or Traffic reports near the top of the screen. Search or filter by place, then select a station, camera or report.

  4. Explore at your own pace

    Close panels when you have finished with them. Use the globe button to return to a view of Earth. Try VISUAL PRESETS or SCENES when you feel ready.

Coverage and update times vary by source; not every view is live. Data sources inside the app explains what is connected. Choose ↖ Dream Unity to return home.
