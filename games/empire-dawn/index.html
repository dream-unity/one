    How to play

    1. Choose a worker. Tap berries to get food, or a tree to get wood.
    2. Choose a worker, then Build. Pick a building. Tap an empty place.
    3. Choose your Main house to make more workers. Build a Guard camp to make guards.
    TapChoose a person. Then tap a place to send them there.
    DragDraw a box around people to choose a group.
    MapOpen the small map. Tap a place to look there.
    ClearPut down your choice.
    + / −See things bigger or smaller.
    MouseLeft-click to choose. Right-click to send.
    Keyboard shortcuts

    W A S D or arrow keys move the view. The mouse wheel zooms. H finds your Main house. B opens Build. P pauses. Escape cancels.

    Hold Shift to add people to a group. Ctrl + 1–5 saves a group. Press its number to choose it again. Space finds your chosen people.
